RPG Project Script — The Forgotten Village
Game Concept

The player arrives in a small village with six houses. At first, the village looks peaceful, but the player soon discovers that one villager disappeared during the previous night.

Nobody clearly remembers what happened.

The player must enter the houses, talk to villagers, solve challenges, and collect clues. Each house reveals part of the story.

After collecting all five clues, the sixth house—the abandoned house—is unlocked and contains the final mystery.

Opening

The player enters the village.

A message appears:

“You arrive at a quiet village surrounded by mountains. Something feels wrong.”

The player walks toward the village center and meets the Village Elder.

Elder Dialogue

Elder:

“You are not from here, are you?”

“Something happened last night.”

“One of our villagers disappeared, but none of us can remember exactly what happened.”

“Since this morning, strange things have been appearing around the village.”

“Please… talk to the others. Maybe you can discover the truth.”

Main Quest starts:

Main Quest: The Missing Villager
Investigate the village and discover what happened.

House 1 — Elder's House
Purpose

Tutorial house.

The player learns how to:

talk to NPCs
inspect objects
collect items
update quests

Inside the Elder's house, the player can inspect several objects.

One object is a damaged photograph.

Player finds:

Old Photograph

The photograph shows six villagers standing together.

But one person's face has been scratched out.

Elder

“That's strange…”

“I remember taking this photograph.”

“But I don't remember the person standing there.”

The player receives:

Clue #1 — The Forgotten Person

House 2 — Alchemist's House

The Alchemist is trying to recreate a strange potion she found after the disappearance.

Alchemist

“Last night, I found this broken bottle outside my house.”

“There was something inside it, but I can't remember what.”

“If you help me collect the ingredients, I may be able to recreate it.”

Challenge

Collect:

2 Blue Flowers
1 Mushroom
1 Strange Herb

The player explores the village and finds them.

After returning:

Alchemist

“Perfect. Give me a moment.”

The potion reacts strangely.

“This isn't medicine.”

“This potion affects memory.”

The player receives:

Clue #2 — Memory Potion

House 3 — Carpenter's House

The Carpenter says somebody entered his workshop during the night.

Carpenter

“Everything was moved.”

“Tools, boxes, even the furniture.”

“I thought it was just a thief…”

“But nothing was stolen.”

Challenge

Inside the workshop, several objects are in the wrong places.

The player must place the objects back correctly.

Example:

Hammer      → Tool Table
Wood        → Storage Area
Lantern     → Wall Shelf
Key         → Drawer

When everything is correct, a hidden compartment opens.

Inside is:

Broken Emblem

Carpenter

“I've seen that symbol before…”

“It belongs to the abandoned house.”

Player receives:

Clue #3 — Broken Emblem

House 4 — Hunter's House

The Hunter noticed strange footprints outside the village.

Hunter

“Someone left the village last night.”

“But the footprints suddenly disappear.”

“Help me follow the trail.”

Challenge

The player follows footprints around the village.

At several locations, the player must inspect clues:

Footprint
↓
Broken branch
↓
Piece of cloth
↓
Strange symbol
↓
Hidden map fragment

The map fragment shows a secret passage connected to the abandoned house.

Player receives:

Clue #4 — Hidden Passage Map

House 5 — Scholar's House

The Scholar has been studying old documents about the village.

Scholar

“There is something unusual about this village.”

“Every few decades, the same event appears in our records.”

“A person disappears.”

“And afterward…”

“Everyone forgets that person ever existed.”

The Scholar shows the player several books.

Challenge

The player solves a symbol / book-order puzzle.

Example:

Four books contain symbols:

Moon
Tree
Eye
Sun

The correct order must be discovered from clues around the room.

Correct sequence:

Tree → Moon → Eye → Sun

A secret drawer opens.

Inside is a diary.

Diary

“The house does not kill people.”

“It removes them from our memories.”

Player receives:

Clue #5 — Forgotten Diary

Main Quest Update

After collecting all five clues:

✓ Forgotten Photograph
✓ Memory Potion
✓ Broken Emblem
✓ Hidden Passage Map
✓ Forgotten Diary

A message appears:

New Objective: Investigate the Abandoned House

The sixth house becomes accessible.

House 6 — Abandoned House

There is no NPC inside.

The house is dark and almost empty.

The player sees objects connected to all five previous houses.

For example:

Photograph
Potion bottle
Carpenter emblem
Hunter map
Scholar diary

The player must place the five clues into the correct locations.

This activates the final room.

Inside is another photograph.

This time, the missing person's face is visible.

The player finally remembers them.

Final Reveal

The missing villager discovered the secret of the village.

The abandoned house contains an ancient object that can erase memories.

The villager tried to destroy it.

Instead, the artifact erased everyone's memories of that person.

The villager is still alive but trapped inside the hidden room.

The player finds them.

Missing Villager

“You can remember me?”

“Then it hasn't completely erased me yet.”

“Destroy it before the village forgets someone else.”

Final Challenge

The player must destroy the memory artifact.

This can be a simple puzzle instead of combat.

Example:

There are four symbols around the room.

The player activates them in the correct order based on clues from the previous houses.

Carpenter
    ↓
Hunter
    ↓
Alchemist
    ↓
Scholar

The artifact breaks.

Ending

The screen fades to black.

Next morning, the villagers gather in the village center.

Elder

“Now I remember.”

“How could we have forgotten?”

The missing villager returns.

Final message:

The village remembers again.

Quest Complete — The Forgotten Village

Credits.
