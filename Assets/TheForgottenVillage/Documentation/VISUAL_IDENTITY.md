# Visual identity

Style: A handcrafted, low-poly storybook village that shifts from warm domestic spaces to a restrained, cool research-lab mystery; keep the existing course-library models.
Palette: Honeyed wood and parchment for home, weathered stone for the village, deep ink/navy for interface and shadow, teal/cyan for live measurements, and amber for evidence and guidance.
Mood: Soft mountain haze outdoors; warm amber pools in the first houses; progressively cooler, dimmer light toward the final laboratory. UI typography and signals use high-contrast parchment, teal and amber against ink panels.
